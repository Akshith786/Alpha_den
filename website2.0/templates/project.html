Snakes and Ladders

Snakes and Ladders is a classic race‑to‑the‑finish board game for two players, famous for turning a calm match into a dramatic roller‑coaster of luck. My python program automatically determines whether a player has landed on a ladder, encountered a snake, or simply stayed on the same square. It also clearly indicates whether the player has faced any issues or special events during their turn. This makes the gameplay smooth, interactive, and easy to follow, allowing players to focus on the excitement of the race to the final square

Hangman

Hangman is a classic guess‑and‑check word game that two players can enjoy together. One player is secretly given a word, and the other player tries to guess it one letter at a time. Each correct guess fills in part of the mystery word… but each wrong guess brings the little stick‑figure character one step closer to being fully drawn

Rock Paper Scissors

Rock, Paper, and Scissors is a classic, quick‑fire game that two players can jump into anytime — no board, no setup, just hands and a bit of friendly competition. It’s the ultimate “let’s settle this right now” game, perfect for deciding who goes first, who gets the last cookie, or just for a burst of silly fun.
